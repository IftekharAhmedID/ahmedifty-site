 About Research Press

I’m Iftekhar “Ifty” Ahmed. Lately I’ve been building Qube and Translucid — secure meetings and the cloud infrastructure behind them.

Before this, I started Brailly, building hardware and software for blind students in Bangladesh, and co-founded Diagnos, an AI nutrition app, at Rice.

I grew up in Dhaka and study computer science at Rice University in Houston. I like building useful things, from accessible devices to the systems behind AI.
