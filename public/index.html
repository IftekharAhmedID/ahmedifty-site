I’m Iftekhar Ahmed, usually called Ifty. I grew up in Dhaka and study computer science at Rice University in Houston.

I build tools for people to work together, understand information, and use technology more easily. Earlier, I started Brailly for blind students in Bangladesh and co-founded Diagnos at Rice.
