Qube

Visit Qube.so ↗

Secure meeting rooms for sensitive work.

I’m building Qube for people who need to show something, discuss it, and work on it together without handing over the entire workspace.

A product demo, technical interview, or research discussion can happen in one room: video, audio, and a shared cloud computer. Participants join through their browsers to browse, code, review documents, or collaborate.

From infrastructure to a meeting room

Qube grew out of Translucid, my earlier work on isolated cloud sessions and protected media. That work supplied the foundation: run the workspace remotely, relay input, and stream its output through protected playback.

Qube gives that infrastructure a specific purpose. Instead of asking a platform to integrate a new security layer, I’m building a complete meeting experience people can use directly.

The work spans cloud-session lifecycle, real-time collaboration, browser media, and DRM-protected streaming on supported devices. The challenge is making those pieces feel like a simple room people can enter and use.
