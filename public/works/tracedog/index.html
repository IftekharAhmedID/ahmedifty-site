TraceDog

Visit TraceDog.co ↗

TraceDog follows the evidence across public records, web archives, and social sources to find what holds up, what doesn’t, and what’s missing.

TraceDog explores how an agent can investigate a question across public information: follow leads, compare claims, and assemble findings that a person can check.

I’m interested in the work between finding a page and reaching a conclusion. Does a source support the claim? Do two records refer to the same person? What contradicts the story, and what is still unknown?

Evidence you can follow

The goal is a report that keeps sources attached to findings and makes uncertainty visible. Public records, articles, websites, and supplied documents become material to examine, rather than facts to accept automatically.

It’s an evolving side project in agent-driven research and verification.
